// 02. SPATIAL METHODOLOGY

Ecological Systems as Structural Drivers

Rather than treating landscape as passive decoration, our framework uses geomorphology, drainage hydrology, and community micro-ecologies as the primary structural spine for architectural decisions.

METHOD_01

Tidal & Hydrological Modeling

Computational water runoff simulations, sea-level transgression envelopes, and biological retention corridors that mitigate 100-year storm surges.

TOOLS QGIS / Grasshopper / HEC-RAS
METHOD_02

Adaptive Material Lifecycles

Employing locally reclaimed granite aggregates, bio-composites, and low-embodied carbon timber assemblies engineered for dismantling and geological integration.

METRICS LCA Carbon Index < 140kg/m²
METHOD_03

Multiscalar Civic Fabric

Iterating seamlessly from 500-hectare territorial zoning strategies down to 1:1 tactile details, street furniture joints, and pedestrian porosity.

OUTPUT BIM / Vector Detailing / Schematics

// 03. PROFILE & BACKGROUND

Designing landscapes that anticipate climate volatility.

Mats Lindberg is a Scandinavian spatial designer and landscape architect focused on climate resilience, ecological remediation, and public realm master planning. Educated at the Royal Danish Academy of Fine Arts (KADK) and ETH Zürich, his work explores the threshold between constructed architectural forms and evolving biological topographies.

He has led collaborative consortia across the Baltic Sea, North Sea Basin, and Singapore strait, delivering climate vulnerability masterplans, community urban greenways, and speculative tidal infrastructures.
